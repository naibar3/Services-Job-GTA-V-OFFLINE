font_data.h embeds the TH Sarabun PSK font (two TrueType files, regular and bold) as byte arrays.
TH Sarabun PSK is one of the Thai national fonts released by SIPA (Software Industry Promotion Agency, Thailand),
free to use and redistribute with its copyright notice. Keep this notice when redistributing.
To regenerate font_data.h, put any other free Thai font in its place with the same two array names
(FONT_REG, FONT_BOLD) and their _LEN constants.
